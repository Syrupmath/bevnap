The backstory

Built by the folks behind some of the world's most successful bar programs — for the high-volume restaurant bar and the devoted home enthusiast alike.

I've been a professional bartender and bar manager for thirty years — and for nearly as long, I've been sharing the tools, techniques, and math that make great bar programs run. Bevnap is the culmination of all of it: every calculator, every workflow, every hard-won lesson about organizing and running a world-class cocktail program, finally together in one place.

From syrup calculations and batch sizing to ABV, pour cost, and carbonation — the tools I use every single day behind the bar are all here, alongside a personal recipe database you can build, organize, and share on your own terms.

No subscription. No lock-in. Your recipes belong to you, and you can get them out anytime in plain text or spreadsheet format.

The tools

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Every calculator in Bevnap was built to solve a real problem at every stage of running a bar — from prep and production to planning and pricing.

Batch Cocktail Calculator

Scale any recipe to a target volume or number of servings — then save the result straight to your recipe library.

Universal Syrup Calculator

From dry sugars to fruit juices to liquid sweeteners like honey and agave — get a perfect 1:1 or 2:1 syrup every time. Takes the guesswork out of prep and keeps your ratios consistent.

ABV Calculator

Bevnap automatically calculates the ABV of any cocktail as you enter your ingredients — essential for building menus, writing specs, and empowering both staff and guests with the information they need to make educated decisions.

Pour Cost Calculator

Know your numbers. Price out every cocktail against all your ingredient costs — spirits, mixers, garnishes, and ice — and hit your target margins every time.

Carbonation & Pressure Calculator

Calculate the perfect carbonation level for any beverage — and the exact dispense pressure your draft system needs to serve it right. Built in collaboration with the experts at Crossroads Consultancy, based on real-world CO₂ solubility data.

Slushy Calculator

Take the guesswork out of frozen cocktails — get the right sugar and alcohol balance for your frozen cocktail machine — so your drinks hit the right consistency every time.
